About Degaga Debelo

Economist and Junior Investment & Advisory Analyst, working where economic research meets capital markets.

Professional biography

I am Degaga Debelo Ajema, an economist working as a Junior Investment & Advisory Analyst at AMG Investment Bank.

I studied economics at Addis Ababa University from March 2023 to June 2026, with coursework spanning financial, monetary, international and development economics, econometrics and project planning.

At the Ministry of Planning and Development I helped collect, clean and analyze national economic data. At the Ethiopian Capital Market Authority I reviewed regulatory documents and helped coordinate the Authority’s 2025 Summer Camp. Together, these gave me a close view of how policy, regulation and markets connect.

Outside formal roles, I co-founded Abyssinia Education Consultancy and started a book club for high school students.
